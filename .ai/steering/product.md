# Product Vision: Bean & Dream

## Core Pivot
Transition from a local physical cafe to a **premium, scalable mobile catering provider** specializing in specialty coffee, matcha, pastries, and craft drinks.

## Value Proposition
The website serves as a high-conversion engine designed to capture high-value event leads (corporate planners, gala organizers, HNWIs) through an interactive, transparent quoting experience.

## Core Goals
- **Lead Generation**: Shift the focus from foot traffic to high-value event bookings.
- **Frictionless Quoting**: Allow clients to qualify their budget and requirements immediately via an Interactive Package Builder.
- **Brand Prestige**: Establish a "premium" and "bespoke" identity that appeals to high-end clients.

## Personas
- **Event Planner (Corporate/Wedding/Gala)**: Needs fast, transparent estimates and customization options.
- **High-Net-Worth Individual**: Values aesthetics, seamless interaction, and a bespoke experience.
- **Cafe Regular**: Still interested in the physical tasting room/headquarters.

## Boundaries
- **In-Scope**: Lead capture, interactive quoting, premium brand storytelling, and client dashboard.
- **Out-of-Scope**: Full retail e-commerce for individual beans, complex real-time calendar availability.
